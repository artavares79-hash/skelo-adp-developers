# Protocol workflow

1. **Establish the deal.** Requester and provider identify the work and explicit acceptance criteria.
2. **Freeze the criteria.** ADP associates the deal with the terms to be used later for verification.
3. **Acknowledge the terms.** The provider acknowledges the agreement before delivery.
4. **Perform the work.** Work happens outside ADP; ADP is not the provider’s execution engine.
5. **Submit delivery and evidence.** The delivery supplies the claims and evidence required by the agreement.
6. **Verify.** ADP evaluates that delivery against the frozen criteria.
7. **Use the outcome.** ADP returns settlement eligibility; a payment rail or workflow may use it under its own policy. ADP neither moves funds nor enforces payment.
